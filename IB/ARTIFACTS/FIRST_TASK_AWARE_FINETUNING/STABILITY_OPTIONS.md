4. **Module-wise or adaptive clipping:** prevent one module from suppressing every other module. [Adaptive gradient clipping](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/brock21a/brock21a.pdf) relates gradient norms to parameter norms; zero/near-zero heads need a suitable floor or exception. Applying this vision result to AC is an experiment.
5. **Residual/attention scale control:** normalization or gates could control AC injections relative to the reader's existing states. [Spike No More](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.16903) motivates residual-scale control in transformers; transfer to this pretrained AC architecture is unproven. Zero-initialized heads are already present, but do not bound later growth.
6. **Optimizer/numerical settings:** Adam epsilon and moments, longer warmup, selective FP32. Lower priority without evidence of numerical failure or startup-specific instability.
