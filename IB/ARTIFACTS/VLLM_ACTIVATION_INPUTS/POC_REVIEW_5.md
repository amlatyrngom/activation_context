## Checks in the requested order

**(1) Bit-identity of existing configurations.**
- *State-dict keys*: `DeltaHead`/`KVDeltaHead` register the same parameters and buffers in both styles (`ac_model_utils.py:534–541`, `:570–579`); `relative*` stay `nn.Parameter`s (with `requires_grad=False` only in the lora style), `rms*` stay buffers; the style is a plain attribute. No new keys anywhere (`load()` reads absent `head_style`/`calibration` as the defaults, `ac_model.py:798`).
- *Forward*: scaled style computes `out = down(silu(up(z)))` then `unit_rows(out) * scale` — the same ops in the same order as before (`:548–552`, `:588–591`); the zeroing of `down` runs only for lora and after the draws, so the scaled draws (and the passage-attention draws taken in a later fork) are untouched. Input-only models never reach any of this.
- *Optimizer groups*: `trainable_parameters()` gained a `parameter.requires_grad` filter (`ac_model.py:494`); no AC-module parameter has `requires_grad=False` outside the lora scalars (grep of `activation/ac_model`), so the group contents and their order are unchanged for input-only and scaled models; `delta_scales` is still built from `scale_parameters()` (non-empty only for scaled heads). Resume layouts are therefore the same as before for every existing arm. The staging code (`ac_model_training.py:529–533`, `:665–672`, `:684`) is inert at `intervention_heads_only_updates=0` (`heads_only` false; `_penalty_active` gains `done >= 0`).
- *Traces*: not bit-identical, by design and documented (§3 of the write-up): the intervention trace now fires every 5 updates below 200 (`_intervention_trace_due`, `:1265–1268`, used at `:689`) instead of 25, and every entry carries two new keys `delta_rms`/`delta_rms_abs` (`:1286`). Training math is unaffected (the diagnostics are 0-d detached tensors drained at the trace, same mechanism as the cosine); the gate trace keeps its 25. Accepted.
- Diagnostics recorded per encode in training mode (`ac_model.py:704–716`) are no-grad, no-sync; `row_kv_rms` in the K/V hook (`hf_utils.py:167–170`) is computed only when rows land (after the `keep.any()` guard), so cached decode steps skip it.

**(2) Training-friendliness of the lora path.**
- At init `down.weight = down.bias = 0` ⇒ delta ≡ 0 (bit-identical reader forward, tested). Gradients: `∂L/∂down.weight = g ⊗ silu(up z)` and `∂L/∂down.bias = g` are non-zero; `∂L/∂up = downᵀ(…) = 0`, so `up` receives an exact zero *tensor* (not `None`). Under AdamW with zero moments a zero gradient gives a zero step, and `weight_decay = 0.0` (`ac_model_training.py:72`), so nothing drifts. The first-step asymmetry is the standard LoRA B=0 init: after update 1 `down` is O(lr) and `up` gets its first real gradient at update 2 — a one-update delay, no pathology. In the passage-attention arms the asymmetry is two deep: the block's `out` is also zero, and its (and q/k/v's) gradient flows only through `down`, so the block sees zero gradient at update 0 and starts at update 2 (see finding 3 for the reading consequence).
- Magnitude: no `unit_rows`, no learned scale ⇒ the delta has no floor (it starts at exactly 0 and the bf16 dead zone is crossed from the outside: the gradient at δ = 0 is exact) and no ceiling other than the loss. Growth from zero is Adam-paced: each element of `down` moves ≈ lr per update with signs `sign(g_i)·sign(h_j)`, which are coherent across the r = 640 inner dimension, so one update at peak LR can move the delta by up to lr·r·mean|h| — a few percent of the row RMS in a single step once warm-up is over (finding 4: watch the first 50 updates of `intervention_delta_rms`).
- Heads-only staging (`:665–672`): after the backward, every non-intervention parameter of the AC group (encoder modules, gates, `target_head.scale`, side LoRA) gets `.grad = None`, the reader is treated as frozen (grads dropped, group-1 LR zeroed at `:684`, no penalty term via `_penalty_active`, `:1143`). Parameters with `None` grads are skipped by `clip_grad_norm_` and by AdamW (no moments accrue), so the clipping norm during staging is the heads' and blocks' alone; `encoder_has_grad` stays true (heads have grads) so the row cache is still invalidated. `set_learning_rates` runs every update, so warm-up and cosine keep their clock; after the staging the reader's LR is rewritten from `base_lr` on the next update (`:682`), and the encoder's group never had its LR touched. Resume restores `updates_done` from `optimizer.pt` with an unchanged layout, so the staging resumes where it left off (tested across a round-trip at update 3/4). Scaled heads under staging keep stepping their `relative` (they are `delta_heads.*`). Consistent with the existing reader-staged recipe.

**(3) Row-position calibration.** `capture_layer_input_rms(…, positions)` records `mean over positions of per-position RMS` of the layer input (`hf_utils.py:255–258`); the intervention pre-hook writes `row_rms` as `hidden[row, local[keep]].pow(2).mean(-1).sqrt().mean()` (pre-delta, same positions) — the same quantity. K/V: `capture_kv_rms(…, positions)` flattens `k_norm`/`v_proj` output to `[B, S, H·D]` before indexing (`:286–289`); the K/V hook flattens `output[row, local[keep]]` to `[n, H·D]` (`:168–169`) — the same. Positions: `_calibration_rows` (`ac_model_training.py:1253–1262`) uses `example.spans` expanded position-by-position — exactly what `_assemble_student` puts in `payload["positions"]` (`:1236`). Inputs: `_assemble_student(..., deltas_off=True)` returns the rows written over the placeholders and `None` payload (`:1234–1235`), so the reader sees rows, no deltas, hooks inert. The encode runs after `set_mode(TRAINING)` (`:479`) under `no_grad` inside `uncalibrated_encoding()` (`ac_model.py:413–423`), whose `finally` clears the diagnostics; `calibrate_interventions` ends with `invalidate_encoded_rows()` (`:487`), so the rows/deltas encoded with `rms = 0` never survive into training through the row cache. No-context reference: it is built from the stripped-history `penalty` examples (`:510`) and the stored reference; the calibration reads `example.student_ids`/`spans` and mutates nothing (fresh embedding tensor, new assembled tensor), so rows cannot leak into it. Mode/inputs mismatches raise (`ac_model.py:438–442`). Note the semantic shift for the *scaled* style under `row_positions`: `scale_fraction` then means 5 % of the true row-position RMS (≈ 0.40 at layer 7), 3× louder than before — no arm in this patch combines the two, and `load()` refuses cross-mode loads.

**(4) `load()` and migration.** A phase-F deep checkpoint (no `head_style`/`calibration` keys → `("scaled", "no_rows")`) loads into a scaled/no-rows config unchanged and is refused by a lora or row-positions config (`ac_model.py:798–801`, tested); a lora checkpoint is refused by a scaled model (tested) and by an input-only config (`saved_layers != []`). The migration path (input-only → deep) skips the style check (inside `if saved_layers:`), expects exactly the deep keys as missing — the same set for both styles — and leaves calibration pending, so NDIAWL/NKVWL/NDIWL/A32DL migrate K16r1 epoch-2 into fresh zero heads and calibrate on the warm reader with the warm rows present. `_convert_absolute_scales` finds no `scale` keys in a lora state and is a no-op. `save()` stores `intervention_summary()` (`:758`), which now carries `head_style`/`calibration`.

**(5) vLLM parity.** The payload is still per-row deltas at `positions` (`Interventions` gains only the diagnostic `row_kv_rms`, HF-hook-side), the head is a per-row map with no position dependence, and the calibration/staging are training-time only. Nothing position-at-a-time was added.

## Findings

**1. LOW — Eval-time encodes feed the training trace's numerators but not its denominators.** `delta_rms` (and the pre-existing `cosine`) are recorded on every training-mode encode (`ac_model.py:704–716`), including the reporting / `deltas_off` panels and the completion samples, while `row_rms` is harvested only under grad (`_harvest_row_rms`). The first trace point after a panel therefore averages a numerator over training + panel rows and a denominator over training rows only. The heads are the same, so the bias is second-order; fix when convenient by recording the diagnostics only when `torch.is_grad_enabled()` (one condition at `:707`/`:711`).

**2. LOW — `intervention_summary()` for lora heads reports an anchor as "scales".** `scales = rms × relative` and `head_parameters` (which counts the inert scalars) are still emitted (`ac_model.py:392–411`); `parametrization: "lora"` marks them, but `result.json` readers should not take `scales` as the applied magnitude — the `delta_rms_abs` trace field is. Cosmetic; either drop `scales` for lora or add `applied_delta_rms` from the last trace entry.

**3. LOW — A2 + lora starts two zero layers in series; say so in the reading guide.** In NDIAWL/NDIAWLS/A32DL the block's `out` and the head's `down` are both zero, so `attention_grad_norm` reads 0 at update 0 and the block cannot move until `down` has (update 2 on). This is fine, but the arm notes promise "intervention_grads must stay non-zero and climb" without mentioning that `intervention_attention` (out-norm) lags the head by design; a reader could call the attention path dead at the first points. Add one clause to the NDIAWL/NDIAWLS/A32DL notes.

**4. INFO — Watch the early growth rate.** With Adam, coherent signs across r = 640 and lr 1e-4 at the end of warm-up, a single update can add a few percent of the row RMS; the heads sit in the encoder group and cannot be slowed separately (there is no per-head LR; `learning_rate_delta_scales` has no group in the lora style). If `intervention_delta_rms` overshoots the 2–10 % band in the first 50 updates and the held panel worsens against `deltas_off`, the lever is `encoder_lr` (or a head LR group — a small addition, not needed now).

**5. INFO — NDIAWLS releases the shared parameters at ≈ 0.9 × peak cosine LR with fresh moments.** The 200 staged updates cover the 50-update warm-up, so the encoder, gates and both LoRAs take their first Adam (sign-like) steps at nearly full rate at update 200. That is exactly what the reader-staged recipe does, and it is the intended experiment, but the cosine clock does not re-warm; the first panel after update 200 should be read with that in mind (a dip there is the release, not the heads).

**6. INFO — `weight_decay` must stay 0 for the zero-gradient stage to be inert.** With `weight_decay > 0`, AdamW would decay `up` and the block's q/k/v while they still receive exact zero gradients (they hold zero tensors, not `None`). Current default is 0.0 (`ac_model_training.py:72`) and no arm sets it; noting it for whoever revisits the optimizer.

## Verdict: APPROVE

Findings: 1. Eval-time encodes feed the trace numerators but not the denominators (LOW) — 2. `intervention_summary()["scales"]` is an anchor, not the applied magnitude, in the lora style (LOW) — 3. A2 + lora: two zero layers in series, the attention out-norm lags by design; note it in the arm notes (LOW) — 4. Watch the early growth rate under Adam (INFO) — 5. NDIAWLS releases the shared parameters at near-peak LR with fresh moments (INFO) — 6. `weight_decay` must stay 0 for the zero-gradient stage to be inert (INFO).
