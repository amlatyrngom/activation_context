## The result this answers

A32KVSn (K/V slots at 1/32, learned slot content from the side states) sits exactly on the input-only control at 0.5 epoch (0.380 vs deltas-off 0.379, A32X 0.377); `intervention_takeover` drifted slightly negative (−0.02 to −0.04: keep > 1) and `intervention_slot_rms` stayed ≈ 1.0. Reading: a learned head that produces K/V from side residual states writes a representation the reader's attention heads do not read natively, the reader LoRA at 2e-5 cannot re-learn its query side within 1,024 updates, and improving the existing input channel is always the cheaper gradient — so the slots stay the reader's own K/V. Side and reader share one base model, so the side model's *own* K/V at the same layer are already in the format the reader's heads read: that is what this round transfers.

## A. `intervention_target = "kv_transfer"`

At every chosen full-attention reader layer l, the row positions' pre-RoPE K and V are replaced by

    K[rows] = (1 − t_k) · K_reader[rows] + t_k · (K_side[rows] + corr_k(side state))     (V likewise, with t_v, corr_v)

where `K_side[rows]`, `V_side[rows]` are the side model's keys (after `k_norm`) and values (after `v_proj`) at layer l at the row positions of the side sequence (`[passage, summary rows]`), captured during the side (encoder) pass — the side runs the rows over the passage with its LoRA, so these carry the passage at depth l. `t_k`, `t_v` are the layer's learned takeovers, initialised at `intervention_transfer_init` (default 1.0 = a full replacement from update 0; 0.5 exposed as an option) and trained in the `delta_scales` group at `learning_rate_delta_scales` (`delta_scale_lr: 1e-3` in the arms), like the slot heads' takeovers of round 5. `corr_*` is an optional zero-initialised lora-style correction head (`intervention_transfer_correction`, default off: `d_side → r → 2·d_kv`, SiLU, `down = 0`, drawn from the same forked stream as the slot head so its `up` equals the slot head's).

**Meaningful at init, by design.** Because the side K/V are in the reader's native format, the replacement at t = 1 is *not* an identity: from update 0 every target token's attention at layer l reads the side model's K/V of the rows instead of the reader's own (tested: the reader's forward with the payload differs from the forward without it at init; the deltas-off panel is the reader's own K/V on the same rows, i.e. the input-only control). This is the opposite choice from `kv_slots` (bit-identical at init), and it is what the diagnosis asks for: no fresh representation for the reader's heads to learn to read.

**Where it lives.**
- `KVTransferHead` (`activation/ac_model/ac_model_utils.py:697-735`): the two takeovers, the optional `up` / `down`, the `rms_k` / `rms_v` diagnostic anchors; `forward(side state) → (dK, dV) | None`; `keep() = 1 − takeover`; `scale_parameters() = [takeover_k, takeover_v]`. Registered under `delta_heads` like every K/V head (`ac_model.py:272`), so staging, freezing refusal, the bench's parameter hashes, migration and the optimizer layout treat it as before. Without the correction the state dict gains exactly `takeover_k`, `takeover_v`, `rms_k`, `rms_v` per layer (tested).
- Capture: `capture_kv_rows(model, layers, spans)` (`activation/harness/hf_utils.py:357-385`) — forward hooks on `self_attn.k_norm` and `self_attn.v_proj` of the chosen layers of the *side* model, recording the `[start:end]` slices of every physical row flattened to `[M, num_kv_heads·head_dim]` in the side dtype (graph-attached, one capture per forward). The encoder wraps its side forward in it (`ac_model.py:785-806`); the spans are the summary rows `[N, N+V)` (`_transfer_span`, `:885`). The capture is inside the side's non-reentrant checkpointed layers: the hooks save no tensors of their own, so a recompute re-creates the same saved-tensor sequence and the captured slices' backward finds its values — tested: the gradients of the takeovers and of the side adapter of a spliced layer under checkpointing equal the direct ones to 1e-6, and the side adapter's B matrices receive gradient through the transferred K/V.
- Encoder (`ac_model.py:857-869`): per layer, `content = t · (K_side[rows] (+ corr(source)))`, `keep = 1 − t`; `detach_source` detaches the side K/V (the control); the `delta_rms` diagnostic is the content's RMS. The payload entry is the **`kv_slots` entry** `(k, v, keep_k, keep_v)`: the reader path (the `add_rows` replacement in the K/V hooks, fp32 mix, `slot_kv_rms`), `_assemble_student`, byte accounting, `generate_with_interventions`, `deltas_off`, the row cache and `encode_batch(with_layers=True)` are exactly round 5's — nothing new runs in the reader. At t = 1 without correction the reader's row K/V are the side's exactly (`own · 0 + content`; tested with `torch.equal`), other positions untouched at the first spliced layer, generation's first-step logits equal the training forward's and greedy decoding equals a full recompute.
- Layer choice: `intervention_frequency = -1` = every full-attention layer of the reader (`resolve_intervention_layers`, `ac_model_utils.py:750`; 0 excluded, the last-layer flag and snapping moot; works for every target), or an explicit `intervention_layers` list (`ac_model.py:123`, with frequency 0; validated against the reader's depth and, for K/V targets, full attention). The bench takes `interventions: -1` (or a list).
- Config validation (`ac_model.py:182-188`): needs `intervention_head_style = "lora"` and `intervention_source = "side_layers"` (the correction reads the side state at the reader's own layers; `intervention_source_layers` may still name other side layers for the correction — the K/V always come from layer l); the side and the reader must be one loaded model (`:318`). `intervention_summary()` adds `transfer = {init, correction}` and `takeover` per layer; `load()` refuses a target mismatch (`source/target`, tested against `kv_slots`, `kv`, and `kv_transfer_full`) and a correction mismatch (`:970`). Migration of an input-only checkpoint gives fresh takeovers at the init value and pending calibration, as for the other targets.
- Trainer / reporter: the trace and the widgets are the replacement-target ones (`kv_replacement`: `intervention_takeover` starts at 1.0, `intervention_slot_rms` = written / own ≈ the side / reader K/V RMS ratio, `intervention_delta_rms` = content / own); the takeovers form the scale group (tested: three groups, the last exactly the four takeovers). Widget texts updated (`ac_model_reporter.py:177-186`).

**vLLM mapping.** Unchanged from `kv_slots`, i.e. plain: per request and per spliced layer, M rows of `(k, v)` (bf16, the same bytes as the additive deltas) plus two per-layer scalars of model state; the write `k[idx] = keep_k·k[idx] + rot(t·k_side)`, `v[idx] = keep_v·v[idx] + t·v_side` at the `Qwen3NextAttention.forward` seam into the row positions' cache entries; at t = 1 the reader's own K/V of the rows need not be computed. The side K/V come from the encoder's forward exactly as the rows do (the encoder is the side model with its LoRA; the payload is what it emits). Nothing position-at-a-time, no kernel, no scheduler or cache-layout change; every-layer transfer (NKVTWA) is the same write at eight layers instead of five.

## B. `intervention_target = "kv_transfer_full"` — CEILING MEASUREMENT, NOT A CANDIDATE

The same splice over the **whole passage**: the reader's placeholder run for a part is `R = max(N, V)` positions (`run_rows`, `ac_model.py:347`; `part_view_rows` returns it, so the study generator, the trainer's spans and the capacity check see the longer run), the encoder's `input_embeds` are the V rows followed by `R − V` zero rows (`:820-823`), and the payload covers side positions `[0, R)` — passage token j's own K/V lands on run position j at every chosen layer (`_transfer_span`, `:885`; the summary rows only when V > N). So at those layers the reader attends to the side model's full-length K/V of the passage (zero compression there) while its input channel still carries the V rows. deltas-off = the reader's own K/V over the same run. Content pooling must be off (validated) and nested parts are refused (`_refuse_nested_parts`, `:353`; a nested part has no side positions of its own). Shares every line of the `kv_transfer` code path — the only differences are the span selector, the run length and the zero fill. It is HF-only and labelled a ceiling in the config doc, the summary (`measurement_only`), the bench summary line and the arm notes; it is **not** engine-compatible (the run is as long as the passage). Reading: the gap between NKVTF / A32KVTF and NKVTWA / A32KVTA is what the row budget costs once the reader reads native side K/V; if the ceiling does not beat the input-only line either, the fetch at these layers is not the limitation.

## E. Decisions / what was not done

1. **The reader path is `kv_slots`'s** (payload entry, hooks, generation, trainer): the transfer differs only in where the content comes from. This keeps one engine mapping and one set of widgets; `kv_replacement` is the shared property, `kv_slots` / `kv_transfer` / `kv_transfer_full` the specific ones.
2. **The capture is a hook inside the checkpointed side layers** rather than a recomputation from the captured layer input: exact by construction (the values the side actually computed), and the checkpointed / direct gradient agreement is tested. If a future transformers version made the side's checkpointing reentrant, the captured slices would lose their graph — the test would catch it.
3. **The ceiling's run fill is zeros** (not a placeholder token's embedding): the reader's RMSNorm maps them to zero at layer 0, so the extra positions carry nothing of their own into the spliced layers; their K/V at the *un*-spliced (linear-attention) layers come from what they attend to. It is a measurement; the deltas-off panel uses the same run.
4. `kv_transfer` requires `intervention_source = "side_layers"` even without the correction head (the capture spans are shared with the source capture; nothing else is read from it when the correction is off).
5. Not done: no GPU run; no vLLM change (the mapping is round 5's); no bench key for an explicit layer list (the config accepts one; `interventions: -1` covers the arms); `kv_transfer_full` is HF-only by design.
