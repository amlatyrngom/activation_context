## What the experiment evidence actually supports

- **Reader shortcut is measured, not hypothetical.** `rag_ac_poc/ABLATION_RESULTS.md`: trainable-reader arm A held gold NLL 0.584 versus its no-context 0.586; exact KL both 0.466, despite fitting train data. Frozen-reader B obtains a small passage margin. This warrants no-context and swapped-content controls for deep interventions; it does not warrant reversing the accepted reader-LoRA design.
- **Preserving a useful input head matters.** `ac_recon/LEDGER.md` 11:20–13:30 and `arms.json:K16/A4K`: the combined gold-target + identity-skip recipe transitions early at ratios 1/16 and 1/4. A4K reached 0.962 held NLL at epoch .125, versus the earlier A4 final 1.506; K16 and its data-order replicates transition by epoch .25. These runs were still in progress in the snapshot. The causal attribution to skip alone is plausible, not cleanly established: recipe comparisons also involve gold targets/fresh initialization; K16G0 was pending. The old conclusion that trainable readers intrinsically fail at ratio 1/4 is superseded.
- **Scale learning can be slow; the best optimizer fix is unsettled.** Review-2 records target scale moving ~0.0128 to ~0.19 and early mixer scale suppression; gate-group arms are mixed. SK versus SKn favors gate LR in the tiny skip arm, G16 trails A16 without skip. Do not claim a universally validated 3e-3 scale LR or copy embedding RMS to deep residual deltas.
- **Schedule is consequential; warmup and decay remain confounded.** Ledger 13:30: MD (warmup 50 + cosine) ends 0.800 versus matched MK 1.581 at 512 updates. This supersedes ledger 13:20's inference that unique passage count alone controls transition. Separate warmup and anchor-scale tests were pending. Keep a schedule-capable training recipe, but distinguish a promising measured combined recipe from settled defaults.
- **No-context preservation is useful; top-k penalties have limits.** B versus P0 records better novel NLL and smaller floor drift with penalty. Gold-inclusive references and exact/live full-vocab penalties are available in the experimental bench, but gold's isolated effect and live-penalty superiority were not established. Preserve the mechanism and diagnostics, not an unsupported numeric claim.
- **Evaluation leakage was measured.** Review-2 fresh and ledger 12:45: near-duplicate held tool windows inflate old numbers (~+0.04 nats on the clean subset; worse for larger source pools). Deep-injection comparisons need fixed clean splits and separate prose/tool panels, not raw historic headline numbers.
- **Mechanical resume has actual evidence.** Ledger 12:26–12:45 SR shows optimizer moments + 64 updates restored and final updates 128. New heads/scales must participate in the same state/provenance and schedule contract. Existing tests/proofs concern previous features, not deep intervention gradients.

## Required training contract

1. Top-level AC encoding returns a typed bundle: input embeddings plus per-layer deltas, each [M,d_target], matching the same rows; recursive encoding retains side-space rows and the existing recursive adapter. Branch from shared summary hidden states before `target_head`. Keep existing input-head checkpoint behavior and offer the source-verified promising skip initialization for fresh training, without silently changing loaded heads.
2. Independent small heads and learned per-layer scales are accepted. Initialize new deep residual contributions small but nonzero relative to measured destination residual magnitude, using FP32 head parameters, normalization and scale arithmetic. Avoid both scale and output branch zero together (dead gradients), and avoid assuming final side states form a calibrated identity at arbitrary reader depths. The input-head skip evidence does not prove a skip is right for deep heads. Final shape/dtype cast belongs at the reader boundary and remains differentiable in HF.
3. Preserve `ACTrainer` differentiable re-encoding versus `AgentTrainer` exact frozen replay. ACTrainer must never fetch detached rollout-cache values during training; AgentTrainer must never re-encode recorded contexts using today's producer. Old-policy logprobs, training/eval forwards, HF sampled completions and serving all consume the same effective intervention values. Reader training updates only its adapter during fixed replay.
4. Bind deltas AND reader adapter to every checkpointed forward, with non-reentrant recomputation. Do not store request payload in mutable model-global state or remove a hook before backward. Shared side/reader bases need separate bound contexts: a pending reader backward must not use the next side encode's adapter or payload. Tests should run two forwards with different bundles/adapters before backward to expose contamination, plus direct-vs-checkpoint gradient comparisons.
5. Position transformations are shared with input-row spans: full-history assembly, continuation token trimming, reasoning stripping, compaction, and padded/packed batching must transform both together. Packed Qwen3.5 must keep GDN `cu_seq_lens` and full-attention boundaries intact. Every output head's delta rows must have nonzero expected gradients, no preceding-token or cross-example influence, and no gradients to frozen recorded tensors/base weights.
6. HF generation is a distinct caller: current `_sample_completions` calls `peft_model.generate` directly. Add a supported HF utility that binds interventions during prefill/cache-enabled generation and applies only currently executing original prompt positions. A forward-only helper is insufficient. Recompute/use_cache=False must replay without reinjecting already-transformed states.
7. Record one immutable effective bundle (scales already multiplied in) per rendered span. Extend the co-design serialization helpers, not a parallel store: per-layer tensors, identity/dtype/shape/hash metadata, atomic relative-file publication, lazy load and release only after durable trajectory publication. Cache byte accounting sums every tensor. Bump producer versions when any head/scale changes. Old input-only checkpoints/trajectories explicitly mean no deltas, never new config defaults.
8. Never let the currently local `RowCache.put` unconditional BF16 cast create cache-hit/cache-miss discrepancy for another serving dtype. Canonicalize all effective payload tensors once at the serving boundary; freeze/clone where mutable aliases would alter an active request. Training keeps original gradients and FP32 parameter storage.
9. The zero-frequency mode must reproduce baseline behavior and load old states without silently creating active heads. Persist resolved indices and target configuration fingerprint; topology mismatches fail clearly. Optimizer state covers new heads/scales with stable identities; resuming config must not silently inherit saved LR/betas/weight decay contrary to requested settings (the POC found this exact bug).
10. Acceptance separates mechanics from usefulness: direct/checkpoint/padded/packed gradient parity, serialized frozen replay, HF/vLLM logprob agreement in chosen dtype, per-layer delta/residual RMS and head/scale/encoder/reader gradient norms, plus clean held input-only versus deep comparisons with same training recipe and memory positions. Also report payload bytes and throughput: n=4 + final means five extra [M,d] outputs (six total including embeddings), not free channel capacity.

## Scope discipline

No module transfer, target-activation-conditioned producer, KV carrier, transformer-per-head, or new compression algorithm is needed. Preserve the existing reader LoRA, and use frozen-reader runs only as controls if needed. Reuse proven checkpoint/replay pieces and port only the POC recipe prerequisites necessary for a credible baseline; don't turn this MVP into a wholesale experimental trainer rewrite. Runtime optimization flags may be gated initially, but neither gradient correctness nor recomputation replay may be deferred for a feature labeled supported.

### Head-size budget addendum

Do not copy today's `RowHead` 4x expansion into all five added heads by default. At the POC's d=2560, its d→4d→d FFN has ~52.4M matrix parameters/head: five add ~262M parameters (~4.2GB for FP32 weights, gradients, and Adam moments before temporary activations). A concrete d→640→d bottleneck has ~3.3M/head (~16.4M across five). Specify a small bottleneck rule and persist its resolved width; the numeric width is an engineering starting point, not a POC-validated optimum. Normalization and learned per-layer scales remain outside that bottleneck.
