## Checks

**(1) B3 — student capture under the non-reentrant checkpoint frame.** `capture_layer_outputs` (`hf_utils.py:427–457`) hooks `self_attn` of the listed layers and stores `output[0][row, start:end].clone()` per contiguous run of target positions (`contiguous_runs`, `:410`); a basic slice and a clone both create graph nodes whose backward saves no tensor (shape metadata only), so the frame's packed saved-tensor sequence is exactly the un-hooked layer's and the recompute — run after the hooks are removed at context exit, and guarded by `if record[index]: return` in any case — reproduces it. The captured tensor's backward reaches `o_proj` inside the frame through `SliceBackward`/`CloneBackward`, the terminal loss reaches the same frame through the layer output; non-reentrant checkpointing recomputes once per frame for both. The clone is the right call: it frees the block's `[B, S, d]` output rather than pinning it (views would have held eight of them alive), at the cost of `[C, d]` per layer. Test `test_distill_gradients_reach_writer_and_reader` (`test_distill.py:146–173`) takes gradients of the term alone with respect to the encoder, the reader LoRA at layers 2 and 5 and (transfer model) the takeovers and the side adapter, checkpointed vs direct, `assert_close` at 1e-6 / 1e-5; the batched path's per-layer terms equal the per-example ones (`:193`).

**(2) The teacher.** `_distill_teacher` (`ac_model_training.py:1245–1262`) is `@torch.no_grad()`, `decoder_forward(..., lora_name=None, gradient_checkpointing=False)` on the embedding of `example.teacher_ids` — the full-context history built in `build_example` (`:413–421`: `distilled = not reference and distill_weight > 0 and in_context_messages is not None`, then the existing `validate_paired_histories` + `prepare_training_history` branch), no payload (the persistent K/V hooks are inert without the kwarg), outside any `capture_*` context of the student. In the batched path one right-padded forward per micro-batch (`_padded_forward(target, embeds, None)`, `:1415`: pads after every real token, no mask needed under causal attention). `_check_target_alignment` (`:1197–1206`) asserts, over the per-token `teacher_positions`/`student_positions` lists, equal length and at every pair that the predicting token and the target token agree — the check the design review asked for, and it runs again for the rows-off reference in the panel (`:1317`). Tests: the capture equals a plain-hooked base forward (`torch.equal`), is unchanged after perturbing the reader LoRA, the batched teacher equals the single one, and the loss is exactly zero for the base on the full context (`:94–112`).

**(3) Bit-identity at `distill_weight 0`.** `_distill_active()` is `distill_weight > 0` (`:1193`); with it false `build_example` takes the old branches (`distilled` false → no teacher tokens, `teacher_cache_key` digest unchanged), `_example_loss`/`_batch_loss` run under `nullcontext()` with `teacher_rows = None` (`:1134–1140`, `:1371–1377`), λ is never computed, `_report_distill_panel` returns on an empty panel, no hook is registered, no parameter or optimizer group is added (tested: `test_distill_training_and_off_is_inert`, state-dict keys and group layout equal either way). The implementer's four-configuration digest is reported identical; the mechanism agrees.

**(4) The loss.** `_distill_terms` (`:1264–1277`): per layer `mean_pos ‖A_s − A_t‖² / mean_pos ‖A_t‖²` (`clamp_min(1e-12)`, fp32), mean over layers, times `self._distill_lambda`. λ (`_distill_lambda_at`, `:1231–1243`) on `updates_done / planned_total` — the LR schedule's clock, restored from `optimizer.pt` on a resume (tested: λ continues `[2.0, 1.525, 1.05, 0.575]` at global updates 3–6 after a resume) — set once per update before the forwards (`:659`). It enters the update as `(objective + drift + penalty + distill[0]) × item.weight × share` in both paths (`:682–684`; per-example `self._distill_term` at `:1140`, batched `results[...]["distill"]` at `:1387`) before `backward`; the penalty branch (`:1150–1195`, inline, no recursion into `_example_loss`) is untouched and cannot overwrite `_distill_term`. The recorded training `loss` stays the terminal objective; `stats.distill_loss` is the per-layer term before λ, averaged over the update (`:752`).

**(5) B1 — the panel.** `_distill_panel` (`:1279–1327`), no grad, on the first `DISTILL_PANEL_ITEMS = 8` reporting items with rows: teacher forward, encoder + student forward with the payload (rows on, with `capture_attention_qk`), and the same reader on the stripped history (rows off) — four forwards per item, ≈ 32 forwards per panel, less than one training step at the arms' reporting interval (1/8 epoch). `attention_shares` (`hf_utils.py:490–511`) re-does the layer's attention from the post-`q_norm`/`k_norm` tensors with the model's own `apply_rotary_pos_emb`, the captured `(cos, sin)`, GQA `repeat_interleave`, `attention.scaling`, causal mask and softmax, and sums the target positions' mass over the row positions; `test_rows_attention_share_matches_eager_attention` matches the model's eager weights to 1e-5 on a K/V-transfer model (the K read is post-payload because `install_intervention_hooks` runs first, `:467`). Modes are restored and dropout re-disabled in `finally`. Correct and cheap.

**(7) The implementer's open points.** Per-example normalisation: fine — the term is a mean over the example's positions, weighted into the update by the same `share` as the SFT objective, so an example counts once whatever its length, consistent with `loss_weighting example`; the micro-batch variant was a refinement for degenerate short targets, which the recon material does not have. Clones rather than views: better (see (1)). `distill_layers -1` and layer 0: `_resolve_distill_layers` (`:1208–1229`) takes every full-attention layer including 0 if the model had one; on Qwen3.5-4B layer 0 is linear attention (full at 3, 7, …, 31), so moot for every arm; if a model with full attention at layer 0 ever runs, its attention output is token-local and would dilute the mean by one eighth — a one-line exclusion, not needed now.

## Findings

**1. NOTE (reading hazard, fix in the notes before launch if convenient) — the ORCD dense arms cite the wrong control.** `A32XD`, `A32XD3`, `A32XDR` are on the K16r1 init (`/home/ngom/...`) but their notes read "A32X (… K16r1 epoch-2 init)" and "read held − A32X (0.322)" (`arms.json`, the appended hunk); A32X is the K16-init node-1 run and 0.322 is its number — the matched control for these three is **A32W 0.340** (the same 0.01–0.02 init gap the ledger records at 21:50). `NXD` and the twins are worded correctly. The runs themselves are right; only the read line is off, and a 0.018 gap in the wrong direction would be misread as a win.

**2. NOTE — Step time and the 27,000 s budget.** The teacher forward is ≈ one third of the side pass per example (no backward, no checkpointing) plus the panel; expect +15–30 % step time on the 1/32 arms. If A32X/A32W ran close to the budget, the dense arms may truncate the epoch at the floor phase — read `held − control` at matched update counts and note the last λ in `result.json` (`distill.lambda_last`) when comparing.

**3. NOTE — Transient memory in the batched path.** `_distill_teacher` runs after `encode_batch` (the side graph is alive) and before the reader forward (`:1372`), so the teacher's transient activations (bf16, no grad, ≈ 3 × 2,000 tokens) sit on top of the side graph; tens of MB per layer, freed layer by layer — no risk on the 96 GB cards, worth knowing if a micro-batch OOMs on a full-context item.

**4. NOTE — `A32XD3` remains the least informative line** (design review (6)); it costs a slot, not a decision, and the queue has it behind the weight-1 arms. Nothing to change.

**5. NOTE — The per-layer `distill_loss` at init will not be 0 in the rows-off sense.** The K16r1 reader's own drift from the base and the missing passage both contribute; the first panel's rows-on vs rows-off values are the baseline for both curves, and the gap at update 0 is the input channel's existing delivery — record it in the results table next to the final gap.
