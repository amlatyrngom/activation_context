     1	"""
     2	Cut the shared prefixes of the resumption benchmark out of a pool run's rollout cache.
     3	
     4	A run qualifies when it passed --lower tokens and ended under --upper (any finish reason; runs ended by the trajectory cap
     5	are out). Length = prompt tokens + every step's tokens, as the 27B recorded them (the 9B reader has the same vocabulary).
     6	The cut is the first step boundary at or past --lower that is not an assistant turn, so a prefix always ends with tool
     7	results (or a nudge) and the next thing to happen is a model turn. A run whose cut is its last step has nothing to resume
     8	and is left out. One file per prefix (steps without log-probabilities) and an index.
     9	
    10	    python cut_prefixes.py --rollouts ROLLOUTS/pc_pool_eval_v1/rollouts.jsonl --out prefixes_eval_v1
    11	
    12	Training side only: --short-lower S also takes the runs that ended between S and --lower tokens, cut at the first such
    13	boundary at or past --short-fraction of the run (entries marked "cut_rule": "short"; the others "window"). Evaluation
    14	prefixes never use it.
    15	
    16	With --split and --keep-first N (evaluation side), the index keeps the first N prefixes in the split's evaluation order; the
    17	rest go to `left_over`, each marked with whether its repository holds one of the kept prefixes (only the others may serve
    18	training).
