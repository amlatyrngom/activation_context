    12	What one training example teaches. The encoder compresses a task's prefix into AC rows; the reader sees only the task head
    13	and those rows and must write what the untuned reader wrote with the whole prefix in view:
    14	
    15	    student view   head + [user: <AC part: head + prefix, tool definitions> + "\\n\\n" + COMPACTION_INSTRUCTIONS] + continuation
    16	    teacher view   head + prefix + continuation                          (the raw text; read by the KL / match losses only)
    17	    targets        the reader's recorded sampled tokens, one list per continuation assistant turn
    18	
    19	The student view up to the first target is the prompt `Agent.compact("")` builds in a live run (activation/agent/agent.py):
    20	system message(s) + first user message(s) + one user message [part, text], the part being
    21	`activation_part("compaction", flattened view, tool definitions)` resolved with a compression target. The flattened view is
    22	head + prefix with content lists joined to text (`strip_ac_parts`) and a head user message past AC_FLAT_HEAD_CHARS cut to
    23	its head and tail (`head_message`), as `Agent.flattened_view` does. Tool results and nudges between the targets are
    24	context, never targets. Messages after the last continuation assistant turn are dropped (nothing is predicted after them).
    25	
    26	Sizes are fractions r of the PREFIX (ac_sizes.py): rows_wanted = round(prefix_tokens x r), and the part's compression
    27	target is the one that gives exactly that many rows for this part's encoder-side length. Per item `info` records r,
    28	rows_wanted, rows (after the encoder's own limits), side_tokens, prefix_tokens and `ratio` (= the part's compression
    29	target, the meaning `ratio` has in the other packets of the bench).
    30	  training   r drawn per item in [--r-range] from a generator seeded by (--seed, task id, item number). Default
    31	             log-uniform: the evaluated sizes 1/20, 1/10, 1/5 are equally spaced in log, so each octave gets the same
    32	             number of examples (uniform would put two thirds of them between 1/10 and 1/5). With --ratios-per-prefix K the
    33	             range is cut into K equal slices and item k draws inside slice k, so the K items of a prefix differ in size.
    34	  held       the runs of --held-repos repositories (seeded sample; no held repository trains), each present three times
    35	             at r = 1/20, 1/10, 1/5 in panel groups continuation/r20, continuation/r10, continuation/r5.
    36	
    37	Limits (the trainer's): reader history 72,000 tokens (both views), encoder input 65,536 positions, and the encoder's
    38	max_view_rows (4,096). rows_wanted past max_view_rows is clamped by the encoder (a prefix longer than 20,480 tokens at
    39	r = 1/5): the item is kept, marked `clamped`, and counted. An item past the token limits is dropped and counted with the
    40	reason; a held item past them stops the build.
    41	
    42	Recipes: --loss sft | kl | sft_match (several allowed). One loss writes the packet into --output; several write
    43	--output/<loss>/, the same items in each (hard links), one recipe per folder.
    44	  sft         loss_kind "sft": cross-entropy on the recorded tokens
    45	  kl          loss_kind "kl": top-k KL to the starting reader reading the teacher view
    46	  sft_match   sft + full_context_match_weight (--match-weight) with full_context_match_kinds ["compaction"]
    47	Updates: 8 examples per update. A pass has ceil(training items / 8) updates; --epochs passes (default 1), or --updates U
    48	to take the fewest whole passes that reach U. Schedule fields keep the trainer's defaults (cosine to 10 %, 50 warm-up
    49	updates) unless set with --train KEY=JSON.

   153	def draw_r(seed: int, task_id: str, number: int, count: int, low: float, high: float, how: str) -> float:
   154	    """Item `number` of `count` for one prefix: a seeded draw inside its slice of [low, high] (log-uniform or uniform)."""
   155	    position = (number + random.Random(f"{seed}:r:{task_id}:{number}").random()) / count
   156	    return low * (high / low) ** position if how == "log_uniform" else low + (high - low) * position
