   111	    A deep model (`intervention_frequency` != 0 or explicit `intervention_layers`) adds, per intervention layer, a read-out in
   112	    which the row's final side state attends over the passage tokens' final side states (PassageAttention, zero-initialized
   113	    output) and a zero-initialized bottleneck head (DeltaHead) whose output the reader adds to its residual stream at the row
   114	    positions, at the input of that layer. Both are exactly zero at init, so a deep model starts bit-identical to the
   115	    input-only one, and an input-only checkpoint loads into a deep model (the heads fresh). The reader-side operation is a
   116	    per-request scatter-add on the hidden state entering the named layers: the same in HF and in an engine.
