# Activation-context canon

Versioned decisions for the activation-context (AC) model, its data and training contract. The current implementation uses architecture version 2; STATE tracks application and runtime validation. Earlier learned-marker checkpoints and pooled-profile measurements belong to slice 3a.

## Frozen decision — Golden reconstruction recipe

Future training and checkpoint selection must build on this recipe: 50M reconstruction tokens from deduplicated tool outputs, code, documents, math and web text, holding out whole source documents/trajectories; Qwen3.5-2B encoder into 2B/4B/9B/27B readers, frozen bases and rank-128 LoRAs; rows/source-token ratios from 1/10 to 1/30; no input/content pooling. Keep input rows plus residual deltas at every full-attention layer, dedicated passage-attention readouts and zero-initialized heads. Load trained heads intact when fine-tuning; do not zero them again.

Train literal gold-token SFT with encoder/head LR 5e-5, reader LR 2e-5, AdamW, eight examples per update, 50-update warmup, cosine decay to 10%, joint gradient clip 1.0, and the existing base-reader no-context KL penalty (weight 1 on a random 25% of examples, scaled for sampling). The current trainer's generic encoder-LR default remains 1e-4; Golden runs explicitly override it. Quality gate: held-out reconstruction gold NLL below 0.2 nats/token; report training NLL, token accuracy, greedy reconstructions and no-context drift. Reconstruction preserves every token; its being harder than selective QA/trajectory compaction is a working hypothesis, not a theorem.

Still open: the exact ratio sampling/grid and whether 50M is per reader/ratio or across the sweep. These are not authorization to run the sweep. Checkpoints differing in encoder, reader width or deep channel are not interchangeable merely because their reconstruction score is good. Prefer compatible completed pairs; disclose historical LR/data/migration differences. Optional pooling experiments in code do not alter this frozen recipe.

## Decision — Compaction learns to continue (2026-10-13; replaces the oracle-handoff target)

The task-aware compaction target is continuation matching. The reader receives the task statement and the history through AC rows, and is trained on the continuation tokens of a successful 27B run: either toward the frozen untuned reader that reads the raw history (KL), or with plain text loss on those tokens. Train the encoder with the reader frozen first; a tuned reader is a separate series with its own tuned full-history reference. Superseded history: until 2026-10-13 the official target was SFT on a fixed 27B oracle summary of the trajectory so far. A summary-recovery run improved summary NLL (0.941 → 0.598) without moving continuation NLL (0.798 → 0.799), while continuation KL moved it (0.844 → 0.787; full history 0.689, recent text 0.811). The oracle summary remains the text-summary baseline and the harness's text compaction; the two paragraphs below still govern how summaries are written and stored.

Oracle and encoder inputs stop at the same cut, before the summary or its `compact` call; neither sees future continuations or future study questions. Save summaries separately from their source history in portable releases, reusable across generator or prompt changes. A recorded runtime segment that already contains its own summary is not a valid input for learning to predict that summary.

Use the short shared `COMPACTION_SUMMARY_PROMPT` in `activation/common/ac_parts.py` for synthesis and the runtime compaction tool. Its five sections are **Summary Overview**, **Progress So Far** (`[v]`, `[-]`, `[ ]`), **Notes/Insights/Lessons To Preserve**, **Immediate Continuation**, and **DOs/DON'Ts**. Keep instructions simple for small models: preserve concrete state, distinguish completed work from attempts or plans, and carry actual user constraints. Length targets belong to the experiment; avoid extensive formatting validation or a mandatory judge pass.

Evaluate compaction by resumption: live resumed runs scored on the task and on continuation tokens used, against fixed full-history, no-history, recent-text and text-summary controls on the same prefixes. Offline continuation NLL is a development signal only (report the first 128 tokens after the cut); summary NLL measures summary recovery and is never evidence of downstream success.

## Contract and model

- **Decision — A part is a nested content element.** `{"type": "activation_context", "kind", "messages"}` is produced independently of the reader, with `tools` when present. Segment messages include the system frame. Rendering resolves `ac_name` and `compression_target` recursively from the reader config; already resolved settings are retained. Resolved parts appear inside message content lists. Children encode first; their rows enter the parent through the recursive row head. **09-26: agent-side parts are depth 1** (`activation_part` refuses a part inside a part and a part over AC_PART_MAX_CHARS); nested parts remain readable in old records and study material. Raw and resolved parts share the same content schema.
- **Invariant — V comes from side tokenization.** `V = clamp(ceil(side_tokens × ratio), min_view_rows, max_view_rows)`. Count children as their row placeholders under the side chat template. Consumers call `part_view_rows` before encoding; encoded rows must match those placeholder runs.
- **Decision — One fixed encoder shape (simplified 2026-09-17, full-featured code on branch `ac-recon-night-20260917`).** Frozen side embeddings → blocked-local mixer → learned adaptive summary pooling (token-count bins; no content pooling, no surprisal weighting, no iterative passes) → one causal side pass with the side LoRA over [content; exactly V summaries] → the row head into the reader's input space (identity skip when side and reader widths are equal). Add one shared marker vector across V plus positional encoding; no per-index marker table. Adaptive bins gather O(N+V) source rows, including valid nonempty bins when V>N. Checkpoints written by the retired encoder options refuse to load.
- **Decision — Deep inputs are residual deltas only.** `intervention_frequency` (0 = input rows only; N interior reader layers; −1 = every full-attention layer), `intervention_layers` (explicit, with frequency 0) and `add_last_layer_intervention` are the whole macro-option set; layers snap to a full-attention block within distance 2. Per intervention layer, a zero-initialized row-over-passage attention block (rows' final side states query the passage's final side states) feeds a zero-initialized bottleneck head whose output is added to the reader residual at the row positions entering that layer (forward pre-hook). A deep model is bit-identical to the input-only model at init; input-only checkpoints migrate into deep models (shared modules loaded, intervention modules fresh). Engine mapping: a per-request scatter-add on the hidden state entering the named layers at the rows' prompt positions. The K/V-slot and native K/V-transfer channels (stronger by 0.02 at 1/32 with a bare reader, 0.10 with a LoRA'd reader at the long budget) were dropped for simplicity: they tie side and reader architectures and need a cache write in the engine.
- **Invariant — Content survives initialization at the correct scale.** Ordinary embeddings and recursive rows are divided by the side embedding RMS before the local mixer. Learned small residual/position scales prevent random updates from overwhelming content. Unit-RMS content/summary rows enter the side decoder at a learned input scale initialized to the base embedding RMS. Destination row heads likewise normalize and multiply by their destination’s learned embedding-scale scalar.
- **Technique — Blocked local attention avoids an N×N mask.** Mixer blocks attend to themselves and neighboring blocks through SDPA. Local mixer/pooling/summary activations use non-reentrant checkpointing for training.
- **Decision — Modes own gradient/cache behavior.** Rollout: no gradient, CPU row cache, optional batching queue. Training: no row cache, caller batches. Encoder updates invalidate cache identity independently of saves. Rollout preparation sets freshly injected PEFT adapters to evaluation mode; a base’s existing eval flag does not imply newly created LoRA dropout children are off.
- **Convention — Profile actual recursive sequence lengths.** Sum per-part content+V decoder lengths, not total source tokens divided by stride. Decoder FLOPs must be summed per part; a FLOP estimate remains approximate and must be labeled accordingly.

## Checkpoints and training

- **User requirement — Training reports use ordinary progress semantics.** An epoch/data pass means one pass through the declared training dataset; subdivision of that dataset into separate trainer calls is reported as blocks. Human-facing progress, checkpoint comparisons and cohort labels use **unique questions × completed epochs/passes**, including fractional passes; do not lead with optimizer updates. Use question presentations (including repeats) when comparing equal training budgets across different pool sizes, and state the unique pool size for every curve. Long-context runs also name the evidence-token budget and passage/distractor limits. Optimizer steps and checkpoint IDs remain technical provenance. Keep global optimizer updates, dataset passes, block-local work and checkpoint identifiers distinct. Show one authoritative whole-run elapsed/remaining budget, and label any local timing by its scope. Phase changes clear inactive preparation/evaluation counters. Evaluation charts show the actual named panels and identify the teacher/reference and token subset; unused charts are hidden or explicitly pending. Label latest-update loss separately from fixed-set loss, and token agreement separately from task accuracy. Validate the visible report through preparation, training, evaluation and completion; checking that files were written is insufficient. This is a reporting contract, not a claim that every existing report already complies.
- **Decision — AC train and public eval use the shared execution-config service.** The AC task getter resolves side/target FLA profiles and checkpoint/logits settings from existing configs; both models and bounded sample generation share one reversible application scope. See [shared execution configuration](AGENT_TRAINING_CANON.md#shared-execution-configuration). Kernel probes do not certify combined side/target/adapter memory.

- **Decision — Trainer owns complete epochs.** Default: 2 epochs in one placement scope; one persistent AdamW/global update clock per AC model spans calls, with moments parked on host afterward. Parameter reinjection requires a fresh/rebuilt optimizer. Warm-up counts global updates. Both trainer train() methods accept reset_optimizer=True to discard the receiving optimizer’s complete moments at a phase boundary while preserving weights, epoch/round and global-update clocks. Enumerate adapter parameters by their registered adapter identity; routing must not disable another owned parameter group.
- **Invariant — KL keeps one initial-reader teacher across updates and exact resumes.** Default eager preparation stores all training/reporting/validation targets before the optimizer exists. Opt-in `teacher_cache_block_examples` instead snapshots the initial reader LoRA on the existing frozen base and generates bounded target blocks from that immutable adapter; fixed panel targets stay resident. Blocks never use the changing student. Both paths store top-k IDs/log-probs (positive k, default 32, including the gold token), remainder mass and gold-token log-probs. The k+1-way KL is a lower bound on full-vocabulary KL; report coverage. `_example_loss` rejects missing targets. No-context preservation targets the plain base, independently of the full-context teacher.
- **Decision — Teacher evaluation and checkpoint provenance are explicit.** `eval(teacher="stored")` requires existing targets; `teacher="snapshot"` regenerates bounded blocks from a verified immutable adapter, including on unseen items. `teacher="current"` fills targets from the current reader and must not be labeled a frozen baseline after training. Snapshot identity binds adapter bytes, model revision, tokenizer/vocabulary and k. A resumable bounded-KL checkpoint needs its referenced `teacher_snapshots` directory and paired optimizer state; missing or mismatched teacher state raises. Eager KL optimizer resume is rejected rather than silently replacing the teacher. Fixed baseline completions are generated once and reused. Tiny-model exact restart and real-GPU teacher/optimizer/RNG restoration checks cover this behavior; independent GPU runs need not be bitwise identical.
- **Decision — KL or literal SFT, existing trainer defaults.** ACTrainer re-encodes raw parts with gradients and jointly updates AC modules/side LoRA and the reader LoRA. KL trains against the stored targets; SFT (the default) is the gold-token cross-entropy. Generic defaults are encoder LR 1e-4 (Golden reconstruction overrides this to 5e-5), reader LR 2e-5 (0 freezes the reader: no steps, no penalty), cosine to 10 % after a 50-update warm-up, one joint grad clip 1.0, and the no-context penalty (λ 1 on 25 % of the examples: the reader without rows held to the base model's stored top-k reference, persisted beside checkpoints). `deltas_off_panel` scores a deep model with the rows kept and the deltas withheld. Every evaluation reports gold NLL per target token for the student and, for KL, the stored teacher (`gold_nll`, `teacher_gold_nll` per item, kind and summary); per-position target log-probs and KL are exposed so a bench can measure the same target subset, such as answer tokens or the first continuation tokens.
- **Decision — Three learning-rate schedules, `cooldown` for staged hill-climbs.** `lr_schedule` is `constant`, `cosine` (to `lr_final_fraction` over `schedule_total_updates`, optionally re-anchored by `schedule_anchor_factor`) or `cooldown`: hold the warmed-up rates and decay them linearly (default) or by a cosine to `lr_final_fraction` over the last `cooldown_updates` of `schedule_total_updates`, so a plateaued constant-rate stage ends in one planned anneal instead of a hand-made anneal recipe. `cooldown` requires an integer `cooldown_updates ≥ 1` that fits after the warm-up (checked against `schedule_total_updates` when the config carries it; a plan computed at call time clamps the decay start to the warm-up instead), the two fields are refused under the other schedules, and every schedule holds its floor past the plan. Rationale: the ½-compression stage (Oct 3, 2026) annealed by hand — constant 2e-5 then a separate 300-update cosine run; a standard mode keeps the recipe digest, the resume identity check and the plateau rule honest. `asdict` writes the two fields into every recipe prepared on this tree — cosine and constant ones too — so a checkout that pre-dates the mode cannot load any recipe prepared after it; sync the trees first.
- **Invariant — Paired histories target every retained assistant output.** ActivationContextTrainingItem holds in_context_messages/ac_messages, independent in_context_start/ac_start message indexes, and one assistant_token_ids sequence per retained assistant message. Prepare the prefix once and append sampled outputs plus runtime dialect wrappers incrementally. Teacher/student predicting positions differ but select identical target IDs. Prefix examples and nested transcripts remain context; input wrappers, tool replies and row placeholders carry no loss. Preserve compatible recorded IDs and incomplete tails; reject malformed pairing and tokenizer provenance. The old completion-only item schema must be regenerated.
- **Decision — Shared bases are explicit.** Side and reader may name the same registered LoadedModel with distinct actual LoRA adapters. Equal model IDs registered under different names remain separate bases. Nested adapter contexts restore active/disabled state and parameter trainability. Layer checkpoint recomputation reinstates the adapter used for its forward; each role applies its own checkpoint threshold, and scopes restore original callbacks. Placement/evaluation/cleanup visit each physical base once. See activation/harness/{module_manager,hf_utils,loaded_model}.py.
- **Invariant — Capacity failures precede updates.** Preflight training, reporting and validation: the 72k configured guard, actual reader capacity and every recursive side decoder’s content-plus-summary length. Oversize raises with item/view/count/limit; it never drops or truncates a training item. Unexpected allocation failures propagate. The old dropped_too_long field remains zero.
- **Convention — Exact full-vocabulary comparisons are a separate, post-training control.** Training and every in-call evaluation use the stored top-k targets; a bench that needs exact KL to the original base or to the current full-text reader computes it once after `train()` returns (the RAG AC bench's final exact controls table).
- **Interpretation — QA gold NLL is not generated-answer accuracy.** Teacher-forced answer-token NLL conditions on earlier gold tokens and can miss wrong free-running continuations or extra unsupported suffixes. Compare NLL only on the same questions, gold wording and token mask. Include source-aware decoded-answer panels for promising checkpoints, distinguish frozen initial-reader baselines from matched current-reader controls, and show defective/ambiguous references separately without silently editing or removing them from the recorded NLL. Reconstruction quality gates do not define universal QA quality bands.
- **Convention — Reporting stays bounded.** Exact small-set evaluation at deduplicated 10% epoch boundaries; optional full validation at epoch end. Four fixed eligible inspection prefixes, at most 64 generated tokens each, at baseline and epoch boundaries; SFT generates only the student. Each reference is the selected assistant’s raw token sequence. Empty evaluations have loss/KL/agreement None, and SFT leaves KL/agreement unset. Retain all sample/epoch records. Large decoded secondary metrics are opt-in bench work. Separate initial-adapter references from final matched-adapter comparisons. Report monotonic elapsed/phase times and dropped-item counts. Each new reporter starts its display at epoch 0; repeated calls continue the same report. Preserve cumulative checkpoint/global-step identities separately.
- **Invariant — HF inspection samples stop at chat end as well as model EOS.** `_sample_completions` copies inherited EOS IDs and adds the tokenizer ID of existing `ModelDescription.eot_token` for both ordinary and intervention generation. Do not mutate shared generation configuration. End-of-text and end-of-turn can differ; special-token-stripped text cannot establish which boundary was crossed. Retain actual returned IDs when diagnosing stopping or token budgets.
- **Invariant — Training diagnostics use matching forward populations.** AC mode `TRAINING` also occurs during no-grad evaluation. Collect encoder delta/cosine diagnostics only with gradients enabled, matching reader-residual collection; otherwise post-panel ratios mix training and evaluation samples. This is detached telemetry, not a change to training or evaluation mathematics.
- **Invariant — A completed epoch is an AC/target pair.** Save numbered `AC_MODELS/<name>/epoch_NNN/{ac_modules.pt,side_lora/}` and `LORAS/<target>/epoch_NNN/` before latest aliases, then publish `completed_epoch.json` atomically. Recovery uses `load_completed_epoch()` and both recorded numbered paths, ignoring partially refreshed aliases. Reject incomplete adapters and mismatched model IDs, widths, architecture or structural config. Architecture 1 learned-marker checkpoints cannot load into architecture 2. Existing or partial epoch folders reserve their numbers. Explicit continuation restores `optimizer.pt` moments, update clock and penalty-sampling RNG alongside the paired weights; bounded KL also verifies and restores its immutable teacher snapshot. Preserve the original dataset order, skipped prefix and total learning-rate schedule; an exact-resume runner must reject missing or mismatched state instead of silently starting fresh moments. Ordinary ModuleManager round semantics for agent training remain unchanged.
- **Convention — Small real-data smoke tests gate execution and retain quality diagnostics.** Check finite training/evaluation, matched held counts, complete epochs, samples and paired saves. Persist initial/final AC and empty-context results with a signed final matched-adapter advantage; a small held set does not guarantee AC dominance. Preserve failed comparisons and do not substitute an earlier adapter to imply an AC quality gain.
- **Convention — Checkpoint delivery uses the shared sync root.** Live reports and AC_MODELS/LORAS pull every 60s; only AC_ITEMS defaults to final/manual sync. Use resumable partials, SSH/connect and inactivity bounds, cancellation with process-group reaping, and visible final retry exhaustion. Healthy progress has no default total transfer deadline.

## Source-faithful examples

- **User decision — Task-aware QA holds out questions, not necessarily the source corpus.** Raw documents, code and tables that also support evaluation questions may be used for reconstruction or independent QA synthesis. Protected questions are defined by the experiment's evaluation/task manifest, regardless of upstream split names: an official training split may itself be evaluation data. Dataset split labels alone never authorize using a question for supervision or synthesis. Development questions are for monitoring/selection; final test questions are sealed from training and adaptive selection. Never put held questions/options, answer keys, solutions/programs, support annotations or task-conditioned evaluation traces into synthesis inputs or training material. Shared source facts are allowed. Use explicit question/request partitions and source provenance. The user prefers unconditional synthesis and quick checks; do not add routine corpus/reconstruction-overlap audits or synthetic-question overlap testing. A document-held-out QA panel is optional and separately labeled. Generator provenance must distinguish independently synthesized QA from relabeled benchmark questions. This clarification does not relax the frozen Golden reconstruction requirement to hold out whole documents/trajectories for its reconstruction quality gate.

- **Decision — Run-result conversion belongs to the study class.** `ActivationContextStudyGenerator` owns the methods that turn recorded agent runs into ordinary `ActivationContextTrainingItem` objects, reusing its model/tokenizer and raw channel reconstruction. Keep synthetic-agent helpers in `activation/agent/agent_utils.py`. Conversion and source-fidelity validation are deliverables independent of deciding or running a training recipe; deferring training never makes the converter optional. Current slice scope/application is tracked in STATE.
- **Invariant — Public compaction history and continuation controls use complete turns.** Split long splittable public assistant reasoning/text into complete Python continuation calls with unique IDs/arguments and matching replies, bounded by the reader’s normal generation limit unless explicitly overridden. Preserve reasoning separately for target rendering and exactly once in shared source text/index offsets; partial source-window slices clear the separate field. Choose compaction cuts at complete boundaries and retain the maximal continuation fitting 8192 rendered tokens across all roles and the 72k total guard. For the continuation-control recipe, every retained assistant token, including continuation calls, is a target. Terminal submit_answer is complete without a fabricated reply; other indivisible unmatched calls are ineligible. The former 512-target cap and immediate/delayed mixture are removed. Recorded runs never use this synthetic split path.
- **Decision — QA conditioning is explicit.** RAG and trajectory QA both support `encoder_sees_question`; the reader always receives the question and the encoder never receives the target answer. Uncontextual QA retains the source task and excludes the study question. Uncontextual encoding carries a short general summarization instruction. Preserve source frames/tools and record the conditioning mode; source-span metadata reconstructs overlapping windows once and keeps indivisible oversized calls explicitly marked.
- **Superseded 09-26 — recursive parent-context channel.** No agent part nests the parent any more (nested copies grew rollout records to GBs and the recursion is dropped by design). A search part is the extra passages as numbered `Passage N:` blocks followed by `Question: <query>` (the trained contextual RAG view); a tool-output part is the call and the full output. History of the former intent: `Agent.nested_in_context` placed the parent segment before new search/tool evidence as a nested `parent_context` part. For recursive contextual QA, encode the question-bearing parent into rows, then condition fresh evidence encoding on those rows; do not also expose the question as raw outer-encoder text. Existing study depth2 evidence wrapping with repeated question text is a different layout. Flat summary training does not establish recursive readout/adapter quality. Reuse the existing nested parts and heads, measure this mode separately, and account for actual work/rows at every level. Keep histories before the target answer and match the full-context evidence. Training support and the proposed mixture fractions remain unvalidated.
- **Decision — Train reading several independent AC inputs.** Include reconstruction, QA and summary examples with two or three distinct inputs and corresponding numbered requests; separate target responses with `---`. Reuse existing source records and preserve each input's ratio and recursive structure. Report grouped examples separately from unique source questions/passages and measure multi-input performance separately. The initial mixture uses about10% grouped examples within these families; this is a starting recipe, not an established optimum. Favor shorter intact inputs: the purpose is to make multi-input reading familiar at runtime, with long-context capacity evaluated separately. If a proposed group exceeds capacity, reduce its size or retain singles without truncating source evidence.
- **Decision — Observed terminal answers use the runtime schema.** Normalize only unambiguous recorded Nemotron final visible answers and S1 terminal answer blocks to `submit_answer`; keep reasoning and literal recorded output, even if wrong. Preserve ambiguous SWE shell submissions. Never inject source gold/reference-patch metadata into trajectory history. Count actual observed tool calls before terminal normalization. QA supervision uses its existing study gold, retaining a separate literal answer for scoring generated `submit_answer` arguments.
- **Convention — Shared references and splits have explicit semantics.** `ActivationContextStudyGenerator.transform_for_eval_reference` is bound to its model/tokenizer, preserves suffix/teacher/target fields and does not mutate inputs. Compaction text reference uses the recursively expanded source tail at V target tokens; QA text reference is oracle gold text. Shared `split_items` allocates held items proportionally by source and excludes shared source-qualified trajectory origins or QA question identities. QA questions remain disjoint on familiar source contexts; do not claim unseen-context evaluation.
- **Invariant — Cache reuse validates the experiment.** The bench checks generator/settings/model/tokenizer identity and payload digest before constructing a study engine. Publish payload before its matching sidecar; a stale/missing/mismatched sidecar invalidates reuse. QA study model is `RedHatAI/Qwen3.5-9B-FP8-dynamic`, using the existing 9B engine envelope and nonthinking chat template.

## Agent rollout integration

- **Decision — Parts share one schema across runtime and training.** `common/ac_parts.py` owns the primitives; `activation_part` produces raw content and `resolve_activation_part` binds the reader; `kind` names compaction, subagent prompt/return, tool output, search or parent context so harvesting expands each channel explicitly. `AgentConfig.messages_input` carries dialect messages; the task is appended last. The old `ac_inputs` config key is ignored when deserializing.
- **Invariant — Requests preserve recorded tokens and encoded row bytes.** The dialect templates part sentinels into placeholder runs. Agent rows are encoded through the rollout queue/cache, cast once to the target dtype and kept on CPU. Mixed vLLM requests ship token IDs, a full-length embedding tensor (zero at token positions), and a token/row mask. Registered AC models enable prompt embeddings when the engine starts. No custom vLLM plugin is required by the measured payload overhead.
- **Decision — Compaction closes a segment.** The soft token budget is a delta from the initial segment prompt. Due compaction demands a lone `compact(summary)`; other calls are refused, with a five-refusal limit. The absolute prefix cap ends with `trajectory_cap`. Earlier segments are recorded under `compactions`; the new prompt keeps the system/initial inputs plus the nested prior segment, instructions and summary. Without AC, the summary carries over. The compact turn has no subsequent recorded tool step.
- **Convention — Channel ratios and ordering are explicit.** Default ratios: compaction 1/10, subagent exchange and nested parent context 1/20, truncated output 1/40, search extras 1/20. A child receives the parent's flattened view before its task and returns its own flattened view (without the inherited parent context) before the answer text. Rendered tool parts precede the visible text, including search extras. The tools produce and record these parts even without a reader AC model; the reader renders every channel when an AC model is configured. Parts hold flat text only (depth 1); a part's size is bounded by AC_FLAT_MAX_TOKENS (segment parts) or the tool's own limits (search, tool output).
- **Invariant — Run records retain segment inputs and row provenance.** Record prompt messages/tokens/spans, per-step messages/tokens/spans, and AC name/version. Preserve raw produced tool results in `TrajectoryStep.tool_results` and program-injected results in `AgentRunResult.injected_input`, independently of rendered messages. `activation_messages_of` can render those parts for a reader; old records without the fields are not automatically upgraded. `unroll` visits compacted segments and children; selection includes the parent's compacted segments even when children are excluded. Simulation uses the same recording/preparation helpers. Resume loads exact captured rows from the record without consulting the encoder.
- **Decision — Harvest whole recorded runs by root selection.** Harvest one item per nonempty actual segment, including roots, compacted segments and recursive children; each recorded assistant output appears once. Text runs compress their complete initial frame and retain raw injected/later channel parts; native AC runs keep their real part locations, including targets before later parts. Teacher expansion preserves channel evidence and invents no compaction reply. Filters are score_1, score_1_or_unscored and all; KL defaults all/unit weights, SFT score_1/unit weights. Custom finite nonnegative root weights are inherited by children; zero is a counted exclusion. Shared origin keys prevent root/child leakage across held/train splits.
- **Invariant — Row files belong beside their JSONL.** Agent capture adds exact final-cast detached CPU tensors to existing span dictionaries, without a folder. The writer supplies JSONL.parent to serialize(base_dir=...), which publishes saved_tensors/<dtype-shape-bytes-digest>.pt and relative row_path references. Deserialize resolves paths lazily against the supplied folder; exports copy/rebase tensors. Only successful JSONL append/flush removes rows from actual retained result dictionaries, including child/segment aliases. Save inside the rollout concurrency permit. Failures, no-cache and intentionally unsaved deadline results retain tensors. Reports expose metadata only. Mechanics live in existing agent_utils.py, without a wrapper, global root or new utility module.
- **Constraint — Completed row release is not constant total memory.** AgentTrainer reads captured rows as fixed inputs, never re-encodes them, and trains only its existing reader LoRA with the existing clipped objective. Width, dtype and source embedding basis must match; missing/corrupt payloads error. The bounded encoder RowCache (4 GiB/model by default), active/unsaved rows and accumulated text/token/raw-part histories still consume memory. Lifecycle and policy details are in [agent training canon](AGENT_TRAINING_CANON.md).
- **Measurement scope — Prefix-cache hits follow hybrid state block geometry.** The measured Qwen3.5-4B setup used blocks of 1,056 tokens with fp8 KV (528 with bf16); shorter prompts had no prefix hits for either tokens or mixed inputs. These are setup-specific test expectations, not universal engine constants. Untrained-row rollout checks prove mechanics, not task quality.

## Measurement scope

- **Convention — No question-answering evaluation of a reconstruction-only pair (user, 2026-10-12).** A pair that has had reconstruction training alone is never a point of comparison for question answering, so its question decode is not run. Question scores are measured on question-tuned pairs only; a reconstruction stage is judged by its reconstruction panels and by the question-tuned runs that start from it.

- **Convention — Training memory checks include initialized optimizer state.** Measure a subsequent forward/backward after Adam moments exist, or an actual paired optimizer resume, and include a representative largest training/evaluation input. A fresh one-update smoke can pass while a continuation exceeds device memory; it does not certify the full run. Use a few deliberately heavy examples rather than a long training run for this check.

- Slice 3a’s RTX PRO 6000 profile measured teacher-forward dominance, roughly 30M teacher tokens/node-hour, cached-epoch cost 36% of live, and k=128 coarsened-KL gaps 0.1–0.6%. These are historical measurements, not guarantees for the new architecture. Its synthetic “72k” rows exceeded 74k and omitted an optimizer step, so its 68.6 GiB result does not prove the current cap.
- Architecture 2 acceptance measured exact 8/16/32k part lengths and a full depth 2 production-trainer backward/optimizer at 71,999 teacher tokens with 512 targets.
