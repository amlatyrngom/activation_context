# Activation-context canon

Durable decisions of the activation-context (AC) work: the part schema, the model's contract, its training objective, and the facts about the data sources and the runtime that future slices must not rediscover. Slice 3a (2026-09-09) established these; the M9 validity runs may revise the ratios and the side model, not the contract.

## Contract and schema

- **Decision — A part is a content element, and it nests.** `{"type": "activation_context", "ac_name", "compression_target", "messages"}` inside a message's content list; `messages` may hold parts of its own. The AC model encodes children first and writes their rows (through the recursive adapter) over placeholder runs in the parent's side tokenization. Every caller (compaction, subagent exchange, study items) builds this shape; nothing else carries rows.
- **Invariant — V is decided by the side tokenization.** `V = clamp(ceil(side_tokens × ratio), min_view_rows, max_view_rows)` where `side_tokens` counts the part under the side chat template with the children's rows as placeholders. Consumers size placeholder runs with `ActivationContextModel.part_view_rows` before encoding; the trainer asserts the encoded rows match.
- **Decision — Rows live in the destination's embedding space at embedding scale.** The heads RMS-normalize each row and multiply by a learned scalar initialized to the destination embedding RMS (0.019 for Qwen3.5); the target reads rows as `inputs_embeds` next to its own token embeddings. `d_model` compatibility is learned, not assumed (see ROOT_CANON's input-embedding decisions).
- **Decision — Modes, not flags.** Rollout mode: no gradient, CPU row cache keyed by sha256(canonical part, ratio, kind, model version), an encode queue that batches concurrent requests. Training mode: gradient, no cache, the caller batches. A checkpoint save bumps the version and empties the cache.
- **Convention — Checkpoints.** `AC_MODELS/<ac_name>/round_NNN/{ac_modules.pt, side_lora/}` plus a refreshed `latest/`; the target adapter trained alongside is checkpointed by the module manager under `LORAS/`. `register_ac_model(config)` registers the side adapter (rank 128) when new and loads `checkpoint_path`.

## Training

- **Decision — Self-distillation with the same adapter on both sides.** Teacher = the target base with its current adapter reading the full text (no gradient, no branch); student = the same model reading the AC prefix with rows; loss = full-vocabulary KL(teacher ∥ student) on the completion tokens. The AC modules and the side LoRA train at 5e-4, the target LoRA at 2e-5. A drift term against the frozen base exists behind `drift_term_weight` (default 0).
- **Decision — Completions are real text.** A compaction item's completion is the rest of the cut turn (the text before the cut is raw for the teacher and compressed for the student) or the next assistant turn rendered as the model writes it (text, then the call in the dialect's format), capped at 512 tokens; QA items complete with the study gold answer. No sampled completions at generation time.
- **Convention — The task rides inside the part.** Every part starts with what the reader is after (the task or the question as a user message), so the compressor can bias the rows toward it; the innermost compaction segment starts with the task.
- **Technique — The mixer is blocked local attention.** Blocks of `mixer_window` rows attending to themselves and both neighbours through SDPA on the reshaped tensor (cost linear in N, no N×N mask). A 32k-row band mask would be 1 GB of booleans.

## Data sources

- **Reference — Trajectory sources and their quirks.** `nvidia/Open-SWE-Traces` (`v1.2`, `minisweagent`): structured calls with JSON-string arguments, tool results as JSON `{"returncode","output"}`, the trace ends with a submit call without a result. `nvidia/Nemotron-SFT-Math-v4`: one `train` split ordered cot-then-tir, boundary at row 285,516 inside shard 7 of 12 (loaders stream only the subset's shards); some `tir` rows never call the tool; the cards name no decontamination benchmark, so the loader checks 13-word windows against AIME 2024/2025 (`math-ai/aime24` keeps the answer in `solution` as `\boxed{}`, `aime25` in `answer`). `ScienceOne-AI/S1-DeepResearch-15k`: Hermes `<tool_call>` and `<think>` blocks, five tools in a `<tools>` block that follows a literal empty `<tools></tools>` mention in the prompt.
- **Invariant — One reformatter.** Loaders normalize their source into the shared intermediate (`{role, content, reasoning, tool_calls:[{name, arguments}]}` + definitions); `reformat_trajectory` alone produces our dialect (`shell(script)`, `python(code)`, structured `tool_calls`, one `tool` message per result, the source system prompt replaced by a short domain prompt in `trajectory_kwargs`).
- **Convention — Trajectory chunks are message slices.** Chunks pack whole messages up to the chunk size without overlap and keep `chunk_messages`; an over-long message is split by its own text into one-message slices. The rendering `role: text` + `[call name(args)]` is what bm25 and the study prompts see.

## Runtime facts

- **Insight — CPU-only hosts must hide `fla`.** With `flash-linear-attention` installed, transformers 5.15 routes Qwen3.5's gated-delta layers to the Triton kernels at import time regardless of the device; Triton then fails on a host without a GPU driver. CPU checks set `sys.modules["fla"] = None` before importing transformers.
- **Insight — Qwen chat templates refuse a conversation without a user query.** A mid-trajectory segment (assistant and tool turns only) gets an empty user message prepended by `tokenize_with_parts`; an empty user message renders fine.
- **Insight — The 4B host memory budget of a 0.8B/0.8B CPU training check is about 12 GB.** Two bf16 bases, fp32 modules, optimizer states and the backward at 1.5k tokens exceeded the 10 GB free on the development container; the check runs with gradient checkpointing and ~900-token items.

## Training throughput and caps (measured 2026-09-09, RTX PRO 6000 96 GB)

- The AC trainer calls `configure_fla_cache(device)` at placement like the agent trainer; the kernel configs under `agent_training/fla_configs/<gpu>/` cover the 4B and, by fuzzy matching, the 0.8B on the RTX PRO 6000 (the profile's dump made those matches explicit entries). A new GPU type or a new model needs one pass of `ac_training_profile --dump-fla-configs` (or the slice-2 dump probe); without configs the Triton kernels re-tune per length bucket and the trainer runs at half speed.
- One training example is dominated by the teacher forward (46–69% of the time; 34–45% of the card's measured 410 TFLOPS GEMM ceiling on HF eager). `teacher_cache_top_k` keeps the teacher's top-k log-probs per completion position after an item's first pass; later passes skip the teacher and minimize the coarsened KL (a lower bound of the exact KL, the same loss every epoch, the teacher's adapter as of the item's last exact pass). Eval is always exact. The gap is ≤ 1% of the KL at k = 64 and 0.1–0.6% (mean 0.3%) at k = 128; the bench runs with k = 128 (about 1 GB of CPU memory per 2,000 items); a cached epoch costs 36% of a live one.
- Caps: `max_example_tokens` = 72k teacher tokens (depth 2 at the default 32k compaction threshold, depth 1 up to a 50k threshold); the compaction generator's `max_total_tokens` = 72k; thresholds unchanged. 104k (depth 2 at 50k) fits (peak 73.5 GB) and is measured, not tuned for. The AC side never exceeds the threshold plus 4k rows; only the no-grad teacher side grows with depth.
- Sizing (live teacher, one example at a time): 0k-8k 3,694 examples/h, 8k-16k 2,037 examples/h, 16k-32k 1,441 examples/h, 32k-48k 948 examples/h, 48k-64k 647 examples/h, synthetic 72k depth 2 426 examples/h, synthetic 104k depth 2 285 examples/h; about 30M in-context tokens per node hour above 8k. Peak memory: 47 GB at 16k, 51 GB at 48k, 68.6 GB at 72k.
- Not worth optimizing in this design: the head chunks (0.01 s per example), the checkpointing threshold (the student sequence is below the recompute-free limit), child batching (the encode is 7–11% of an example). The next 2× is a prefill engine for the teacher (vLLM prompt log-probs are the cached top-k), a slice-3b-sized change.
- The three key tests (`test_basic_agent_ac_training.py --gpu --slow`) take about 14 minutes on the node; the item cache for 2,200 compaction items is 300 MB and generates in about 4 minutes without an engine.
## Training practice (both trainers, review pass 2026-09-09)

- One AdamW per adapter (agent trainer) or per AC model (AC trainer), created on first use and kept across `train()` calls with its moments parked on the host between rounds (`persistent_optimizer` / `optimizer_state_to` in `agent_training_utils.py`); a fresh optimizer per round would restart Adam's bias correction every round and open each round with sign-like steps at the full rate. The optimizer is rebuilt (with a printed note) only when the parameter objects change, e.g. an adapter re-injected from a checkpoint.
- Linear learning-rate warm-up over `warmup_updates` counted across rounds (`set_learning_rates`); the AC bench uses 10 updates, the agent trainer's default is 0 (the rollout loop sets it).
- Dropout stays off through a mid-round eval (`disable_dropout` after `model.train()`), and an eval reports how many items it dropped for length.
- Held-out sets are stratified by source (exact shares, not the expectation of a shuffle) and share no origin (trajectory, study question) with the training items (`split_items` in the AC bench).
